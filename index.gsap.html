Questions businesses ask first

Who will we actually be working with?

Before anything is signed, you meet the founding team and the person accountable for building your capability.

Who handles regulated work such as tax, audit or legal?

Specialist professional firms and qualified experts do. Edish remains your main point of contact and leads the build, while the specialists stay responsible for their own regulated work.

Can we see examples of your work?

Where a client has approved it, we share case studies and introduce you to people we have worked with.

How do you keep quality and delivery on track?

Responsibilities, quality checks, reporting and escalation are agreed in writing before the team starts, and reviewed with you as it grows.

Do we need to know which model we want before we talk?

No. Only your name and email are required on the form. We will help you work out the model that suits your requirements, your scale and how much you want to own.

Can we move from one model to another later?

Yes. There is no set path from one model to the next, so you can start wherever makes sense for your business and change the model as your needs change.

Do you only work in Jaipur?

No. Jaipur is one example. We work across India, and where we suggest you build depends on talent, skills, costs, infrastructure, leadership availability and what your business needs.

Do we have to come to India to set things up?

Not necessarily. For an ODC, everything can be set up over video calls.

What happens after I send the form?

Our sales team reads it and contacts you by email or phone to talk through your requirements. We then prepare a detailed proposal, and it can take more than one call to get there.
